PRIORITIZED INDEPENDENT PROBES
1. Reproduce the four known gaps in the accompanying Independent_Review.txt.
   The original executable probe remains at
   tmp/audio_independent_review/probes.py (run as a module from repo root).
   Check each finding against current source before marking it unresolved.
   a. One-column EEG against a two-channel model must be rejected before NumPy
      broadcasting. Also test reordered channels, wrong rate, wrong reference,
      different preprocessing contract and invalid metadata/normalization shapes.
   b. Inject ReplayFailure with a fake audio worker. Record whether playback
      stops or continues neutral; distinguish offline rendering from stream().
   c. Compare direct replay() and CLI held-out enforcement. Training-data replay
      is useful for debugging but is not held-out evaluation.
   d. Compare metric duration against actual rendered sample count, including a
      partial final block. Known-duration totals must have a defined interval end.

2. Alignment and continuous preprocessing
   Compare whole-input and irregular-chunk processing on the same audio, at
   44.1 and 48 kHz. Check common-grid envelope timestamps and EEG pairing at
   boundaries. Test missing audio, delayed availability, discontinuities, reset,
   buffer eviction and recovery. Confirm a window never uses unavailable audio
   or EEG spanning a rejected segment. Separate signal time, evidence end,
   availability time and wall-clock time; a virtual replay is not a latency test.
   Probe zero/negative chunk sizes only in a subprocess with a short timeout;
   malformed generator inputs must not hang the entire review.

3. Decoder and data splitting
   Verify lag direction with a known delayed relationship, no wraparound, exact
   usable target count, and exclusion of unknown labels. Check model save/load
   produces equivalent scores. Corrupt metadata and numeric arrays deliberately;
   retain allow_pickle=False. Check training-only EEG normalization, separate
   validation for alpha selection, and test exclusion from tuning. Reused stimulus
   excerpts need the same group even across participants/files; identity checks
   cannot infer hidden overlap. Subject-disjoint evaluation is a separate choice.

4. Balanced and null controls
   Existing synthetic fixtures always attend A. Add separate deterministic B and
   within-trial switching fixtures with known alignment, plus unrelated EEG,
   shuffled labels and mismatched candidate controls. Use fixed seeds and avoid
   inferring accuracy from the fixture's chosen frequencies. Check candidates
   can be swapped with corresponding truth changes. Report neutral coverage,
   incorrect suppression, switch delay and missed switches beside accuracy.

5. Controller and mixer
   Test ties, small margins, nonfinite scores, stale/future/out-of-order evidence,
   invalid windows, manual override and expiry without new estimates. Scores
   are correlations, not calibrated probabilities. Verify equal-gain neutral,
   requested attenuation, smooth gain changes, finite output and peak bounds.
   Report both commanded choice and acoustic ramp behavior where relevant.

6. Thread ownership and lifecycle
   Use deterministic fake clocks/OutputStream objects. Exercise latest-estimate
   replacement, slow inference, callback exception, worker exception, stop during
   playback, repeat initialize/stream and bounded joining. A blocking device write
   can prevent timely expiry; document that limitation rather than assuming a
   per-block check is a device-level guarantee. LiveAuditoryAdapter is an adapter,
   not a complete synchronized acquisition/audio runner.

7. Metrics and importers
   Hand-calculate tiny examples containing neutral decisions, unknown truth,
   switches and a partial final block. Establish whether switch delay requires
   sustained correctness (currently first match) and whether neutral transitions
   count as selection changes. Use small generated MAT fixtures and mocked CNT
   readers to test units, channel order, audio candidate mapping, crop offsets,
   event timing and unknown-label masking. Do not silently invent trigger maps.
